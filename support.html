Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?
Open MyOlive, go to Profile, and choose Delete account. We'll email you a confirmation link. Once you tap it, your profile and all associated health data are permanently removed from our systems.

Is my health data private?
Yes. MyOlive never sells or shares your data with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect and how it's protected.

I found a bug or the app crashed — what should I do?
Email us at support@myolivehealth.com with a short description of what happened and your device/iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.

Can I request a feature?
Absolutely, and we encourage you send it our way! MyOlive is actively developed and user feedback shapes the roadmap.

Response time

We typically respond to support emails within 1–2 business days.
