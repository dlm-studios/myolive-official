Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 16, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 3, 2026

MyOlive is a personal wellness app. We built it to help you understand your health; not to profit from your data. This policy explains plainly what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Who we are

MyOlive (we, our, or us) is an independent wellness application available in the United States, as an iOS app and in a web browser at app.myolivehealth.com. Both use the same account and data. Our website is myolivehealth.com.

What we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's functionality:

  • Account information — your name, email address, username, and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Health and cycle data — period logs, cycle predictions, symptoms, and related tracking entries you create.
  • Health conditions — any conditions you choose to add to your profile (for example, diabetes, pregnancy, or breastfeeding), stored with your account.
  • Mental health data — PHQ-2 check-in responses, mood diary entries (including intensity ratings and trigger tags), journal entries, and weekly reflections you record.
  • Fitness data — workout sessions, exercise types, sets, reps, activity logs, and workouts written to Apple Health with your permission.
  • Nutrition data — food entries, meal logs, and nutrition information you record.
  • Supplement and medication data — supplement names, dosages, reminder times, and taken/untaken status; medication names, dosages, and reminder schedules.
  • Birth control data — pill name and reminder preferences, if you choose to enable this feature.
  • Body metrics — weight and related measurements you choose to log.
  • Hydration data — water intake logs.
  • Apple Health data — steps, sleep hours and stages, heart rate, HRV, and active calories, read from Apple Health only with your explicit permission. We do not store raw HealthKit data on our servers; it is processed on your device to generate insights.
  • Diary media — photos, videos, and voice memos you attach to journal entries, stored securely in AWS S3.
  • Profile information — your avatar color, bio, birthday, and profile photo if you choose to upload one.
  • Notification preferences — your settings for reminders such as water, supplement, medication, and cycle alerts.
  • Crash reports — if the iOS app crashes, a crash report (such as your device model, iOS version, and what the app was doing when it failed) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so we can fix bugs.

That's it. We do not collect usage analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app and the crash reports described above.

How we use your data

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide and operate the app's core features, such as for tracking, logging, reminders, and insights.
  • Authenticate your account securely.
  • Send transactional emails, including for account verification, password reset, sign-in codes, username reminders, and account-deletion confirmation. Reminders you turn on (such as water, supplement, or medication reminders) are delivered as notifications on your device.
  • Sync your data across sessions and devices.
  • Generate wellness insights on your device by comparing Apple Health data (such as sleep, steps, and HRV) with your mood and check-in entries. This happens on your device, and those Apple Health values are not sent to our servers. No insights are shared with third parties.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating MyOlive for your benefit.

Using MyOlive on the web

The website at app.myolivehealth.com works like the app, with a few differences:

  • Same account, same data — what you enter on the website is stored with your account and appears in the app, and the other way around.
  • One cookie — the website sets a single, strictly necessary sign-in cookie (HttpOnly and Secure) that is used only to keep you signed in. We use no analytics, advertising, or third-party cookies, and the website loads no third-party scripts.
  • Data kept with your account for the website — so the same information appears on the website, your workout history, health log check-ins, logged nutrient amounts, and exercise regimen are stored with your account on our servers.
  • Kept in your browser only — the nutrient profile you enter on the website (such as sex, activity level, and pregnancy status) stays in your browser until you close the tab and is not sent to our servers.
  • No Apple Health data — Apple Health is available only in the iOS app. The website does not use Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Reports and share images — the cycle report for your doctor, its spreadsheet download, and share images are created in your browser. We do not receive the files you print, save, or share.
  • Food lookups — on the website, food searches go through our servers to USDA FoodData Central, and barcode lookups go from your browser to Open Food Facts.

How we share your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties for their own purposes.

We share data only with the following service providers who process it on our behalf, under strict data processing terms:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — cloud infrastructure, database hosting, and hosting for the website (Amazon S3 and CloudFront). Your data is stored on AWS servers in the United States.
  • Amazon S3 (AWS) — secure cloud storage for the photos, videos, and voice memos you attach to journal entries. Media is stored in a private bucket with server-side encryption and accessed only via short-lived pre-signed URLs.
  • Amazon Simple Email Service (AWS) — transactional email delivery for account verification, password resets, sign-in codes, username reminders, and account-deletion confirmations.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reporting. It receives crash reports (device model, iOS version, and what the app was doing when it failed) so we can fix bugs. It is not used for analytics or advertising.
  • Apple HealthKit — MyOlive reads health data (steps, sleep, heart rate, HRV, active calories) and writes workout data to Apple Health only with your explicit permission. Data exchanged with HealthKit is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings. We do not transmit raw HealthKit data to our servers.

Food lookups. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent from your device to the public USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts databases to look up nutrition information. As with any web request, your IP address is visible to them. We don't send your name, email, or other account details with these requests.

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored in a secure PostgreSQL database hosted on AWS in the United States. We protect it with:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit between your device and our servers.
  • bcrypt hashing for all stored passwords.
  • Network-level security controls limiting database access to our application servers only.
  • Server-side encryption for diary photos and videos stored in AWS S3, accessed via short-lived pre-signed URLs that expire automatically.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We take reasonable and industry-standard precautions to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your personal information and all associated health data from our systems. Deletion is processed immediately upon confirmation of your request through the app or the website.

Backups may retain data for a short additional period (up to 30 days) before permanent removal.

Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update or correct inaccurate information directly in the app or by contacting us.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app or on the website.
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format by contacting us.

Children's privacy

MyOlive is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 16 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Users who are 16 or 17 should review this policy with a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. Your continued use of MyOlive after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
