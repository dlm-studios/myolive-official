How MyOlive works

Everything that shapes how you feel, in one place.

🧠

Check in with your mind

Log how you feel and what's behind it, journal with words, voice, or video, take a weekly check-in, and breathe through the hard moments.

🌿

Care for your body

Track food, water, movement, your cycle, and medications right alongside your mood, instead of in five different apps.

🔗

See how they connect

Notice the patterns between how you live and how you feel. On iPhone, MyOlive compares your sleep, steps, and heart-rate variability from Apple Health with your mood.

What's inside

One place for working on yourself.

📓

Mood & journal

Log how you feel and what contributed, then write, record a voice memo, or film a video. A private space to process your day.

🌤️

Weekly check-ins

A two-minute check-in on your mood, sleep, movement, and stress, so you can see how you're really doing over time.

🌸

Guided breathing

Box breathing and 4-7-8 breathing to slow things down when everything feels like too much.

💬

Affirmations

Start the day with words that steady you, including ones you write yourself.

🌙

Cycle tracking

Log your period and symptoms and see your cycle next to your mood and energy, with clear predictions and no selling your data.

🥗

Nutrition tracking

Log meals and monitor nutrition without obsession. MyOlive shows you patterns, not judgment.

💧

Hydration

Set a daily water goal and get gentle nudges through the day. Small habits, real difference.

🏋️

Movement

Build and run workouts, keep your streak, and see how moving more changes how you feel.

💊

Medication & supplement reminders

Set reminders for medications, vitamins, supplements, and birth control, with a history of what you've actually taken.

🔥

Habit tracking

Build daily or weekly habits with streaks, a check-in calendar, and reminders that keep you honest without the guilt trip.

📷

Barcode scanning

Scan a food or drink to log it instantly, nutrition facts included. MyOlive even catches water bottles and logs them as hydration.

🔒

Private by design

Your health data stays yours. No ads. No selling. No sharing. Encrypted in transit, always.

MyOlive supports your wellbeing; it isn't a substitute for professional care. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency.
